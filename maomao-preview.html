Character file

A sharp mind behind the mystery

Raised in the red light district, Maomao keeps a low profile and relies on her practical knowledge as an apothecary. Her sharp instincts and understanding of poisons and medicinal ingredients draw her into cases inside the Imperial Palace.

She becomes an unofficial detective, solving problems others overlook while trying not to attract more attention than necessary.
